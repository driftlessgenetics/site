---
title: Shipping and returns
description: How and when Driftless Genetics ships live liquid cultures, and what to do if one arrives damaged.
---

Orders ship Monday to Wednesday, so live cultures do not sit in a carrier warehouse over a weekend. Most orders ship within 3 business days.

Cultures are alive and cannot be returned. If your culture arrives contaminated or damaged, email a photo within 7 days of delivery and we will replace it.
