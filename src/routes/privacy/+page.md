---
title: Privacy
description: What Driftless Genetics collects, who processes it, and how to delete your data.
---

We collect your email, name, and shipping address to process orders and, if you sign in with Google, the email on your Google account. If you join our email list, we also store that address and send you a discount code.

Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card number. Email is sent through Resend, and the site is hosted on Cloudflare.

We do not sell your data. Unsubscribe from any email with its unsubscribe link, or email jonas@driftlessgenetics.co to delete your data.
